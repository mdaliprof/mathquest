Privacy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short

  • Math Quest has no accounts, no ads and no trackers.
  • We do not ask for a child's full name, address, phone number or email.
  • A photo is optional and off by default. A parent must turn it on, and the photo never leaves the device.
  • Progress stays on your own device. We cannot see it.

What the app saves on the device

  • A nickname (up to 12 letters) and an animal picture.
  • Stars, trophies, scores and which topics are going well.
  • Sound on or off, and the child's class.
  • Only if a parent turns on photo avatars: one small picture of the child, plus the frame and sticker the child chose.

This is kept in the browser's own storage on the device. It is not sent to us or anyone else. Clearing the browser's site data, or using "Erase progress" in the Parent corner, deletes it.

Photo avatars (optional)

  • Off until a parent turns it on in the Parent corner, after a sum only grown-ups solve.
  • "Take a selfie" opens the phone's own camera app, and "Choose a photo" opens the gallery. The app gets only the one picture you pick. It never has live access to the camera.
  • The picture is cropped to a small square and saved on this device only. Details hidden inside the original photo, such as where it was taken, are not kept.
  • The photo is not sent to us or anyone else, and it is left out of backup files unless a parent ticks the box to include it.
  • "Remove my photo", turning photo avatars off, and "Erase progress" each delete the photo. The animal picture is used again.

Backups

The Parent corner can save a backup file to your device and load it back. The file stays where you put it. A backup does not contain the photo unless a parent chooses to include it, so keep any backup with a photo private. Only load backup files that you made yourself.

Sharing

The Share button shares only the link to the app. It never shares progress.

Sound and read-aloud

Read-aloud uses the voices already on the device. The app does not use the microphone or location. The only camera use is the optional photo avatar described above.

The website host

The app is delivered as a website. Like any website, the host (Netlify) may keep ordinary technical logs, for example the time of a visit and an internet address, to keep the site running and safe. These logs do not include anything the child does inside the app.

Children

The app is made for children, to be used with a parent or guardian nearby. Because nothing is sent to us, there is nothing to give, change or delete on our side. Parents control everything on the device, including the optional photo, which they can delete at any time.

Questions and content

Questions are written for practice and fun. If you find a mistake, please tell the person who gave you the app, and they can pass it on to the app's maker. Math Quest is not connected to, or endorsed by, any exam board or school.

Changes
