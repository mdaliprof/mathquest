## Common themes found
**Class 1:** numbers to 20 then 100, number names, add/subtract to 10 then 100, tables 2-5, shapes, time, money, fractions; EVS: body, food, clothes, school, neighbourhood, animals, plants, air, water; English: singular/plural, punctuation, masculine/feminine, present/past, adjectives, articles; Computer: parts, mouse, paint.
**Class 2:** numbers to 999, add, subtract, geometry, tables 2-12, multiplication, division, time, measurement, money; English: sentence, capitals/full stops, nouns, pronouns, adjectives, articles, prepositions, verbs, adverbs, joining words, question words; EVS: body, safety, people, places, food, animals, plants, water, air, sun-moon-earth, time and directions; GK: flags, folk dances, important days, musical instruments; Computer: input/output, mouse, keyboard, paint, files, coding thinking.
**Class 3:** numbers, mental maths, add, subtract, multiplication, time, division, fractions, shapes (2D/3D), measurement, money.
**Class 4:** numbers to six digits, Roman numerals, measures, time, data, simple probability, geometry, fractions, money; Science: body, animals, plants, basic physics; Social: past civilisations, geography, maps, democracy; Computer: software and tools.
**Pre-School (LKG/UKG):** NOT found from a reliable source. Content for Pre-KG to UKG uses universal early-years skills (letters/sounds, counting 1-20, shapes, colours, body parts, animals) and is marked unverified.

## Class 1 update (25 Oct 2026): NCERT + EuroSchool, as requested
**Official (NCERT, ciet.ncert.gov.in/audio-book/118): "Joyful Mathematics 1", 13 chapters:**
1 Finding the Furry Cat!, 2 What is Long?, 3 Mango Treat, 4 Making 10, 5 How Many?, 6 Vegetable Farm, 7 Lina's Family, 8 Fun with Numbers, 9 Utsav, 10 How do I Spend my Day?, 11 How Many Times?, 12 How Much Can We Spend?, 13 So Many Toys.
**What each chapter teaches** comes from EuroSchool's blog (euroschoolindia.com/blogs/cbse-class-1-maths-syllabus), a secondary source: 1 matching/sorting/more-less/big-small; 2 flat and solid shapes, position words; 3 numbers 1-9; 4 numbers 10-20, tens and ones; 5 add/subtract single digits with objects; 6 add/subtract to 20; 7 numbers 21-99; 8 two-digit add/subtract; 9 patterns; 10 time (parts of day, days of week); 11 non-standard measures (spans, steps, cups); 12 money (notes and coins); 13 picture graphs.
**NCERT English "Mridang" Class 1 (via Vedantu):** Unit 1 My Family and Me (Two Little Hands, Greetings); Unit 2 Life Around Us (Picture Time, The Cap-seller and the Monkeys, A Farm); Unit 3 Food (Fun with Pictures, The Food We Eat); Unit 4 Seasons (The Four Seasons, Anandi's Rainbow). I use only the THEMES (family, greetings, farm, food, seasons, colours); no story text is copied.
**Not found:** an NCERT EVS book for Class 1, and a detailed EuroSchool Class 1 topic page (their site only gives general lines: counting, add/subtract, patterns; plants, animals, humans, environment). School-level lists (Burn Hall) add tables 2-5, which NCERT Class 1 does not have, so tables are marked "ICSE extra".
